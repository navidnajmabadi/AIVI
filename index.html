Is my data safe?

The audit sends your responses and contact details only when you submit it. Do not include client, employee or financial information. Before connecting a workflow, review the required account permissions and the relevant third-party data terms.

Do I need to be technical?

You do not need to code, but someone must connect the chosen accounts and follow the setup checklist. The recommendation shows what is required before you choose a pack.

Do I need a paid Claude plan?

Some packs require a paid Claude plan and specific connectors. We identify those requirements in your workflow recommendation before you buy or connect anything.

What if my team will not use it?

Each pack includes a handover guide, example inputs and outputs, and an approval checklist. Start with one workflow that removes a real bottleneck, then expand only if the team uses it.
